### SLIDE 2: The Core Problem & The Trust Deficit
* **Heading:** 01 / The Core Problem
* **Title:** The E-Commerce Market Failure: We Buy on Promises, Regret in Month 5
* **Key Insights:**
  1. **Retailer-Side Conflict of Interest:** Store assistants optimize for cart conversion. They will never tell a user to WAIT for a festive discount, never recommend competitor marketplaces, and never flag known motherboard or transmission defects.
  2. **The Review Honeymoon Paradox:** Over 85% of reviews are posted in week one ('Unboxing was great!'). True product regret strikes in month five—when batteries degrade, thermal throttling starts, and maintenance costs compound after the return window has expired.
  3. **The $25 Trust Deficit:** 42% of online shoppers refuse to trust an AI recommendation over $25 without manual cross-checking (Product.AI Trust Report, n=1,463). Generic LLMs hallucinate numbers and parrot marketing fluff.

> **Speaker Script:**
> *"The fundamental flaw in modern e-commerce is the misalignment of incentives. Retailer bots want your money today. Reviews are written during the honeymoon phase. By month five, when the soldered RAM runs out or the car's real mileage is 30% below ARAI claims, the buyer is trapped. 42% of consumers don't trust AI with their money—because AI has been acting like a salesman instead of an auditor."*

### SLIDE 4: System Architecture — "Code Calculates, AI Interprets"
* **Heading:** 03 / System Architecture
* **Title:** Truth Protection: Deterministic Engine Calculates, Cloud LLM Interprets
* **Three-Stage Pipeline:**
  1. **Retrieval & Research (Queries A–H):**
     * Automatically constructs 8 targeted search vectors: Discovery, Specs, Benchmarks, Failures, Regret, Retail Comps, Lifecycle, and Refurbished.
     * Unified router (Tavily + Brave + DuckDuckGo Lite fallback) with 4-Tier Source Hierarchy (Manufacturer > Lab Benchmarks > Owner Communities > Web Aggregators).
  2. **Deterministic Calculation Engine:**
     * Code decides—not statistical tokens. Strict mathematical formulas (0–100), hard constraint gates (e.g. 16GB RAM minimum), and 5-year TCO formulas.
  3. **Cloud LLM Synthesis:**
     * Powered by Groq Cloud (GPT-OSS 120B) at 500 tokens/sec and Google Gemini (gemini-2.5-flash) with strict native JSON mode.
     * Zero hallucination: The LLM is injected with the structured Evidence Ledger and forbidden from inventing numbers or prices.

> **Speaker Script:**
> *"How do we guarantee truth? We separate calculation from interpretation. LLMs are notorious for hallucinating prices and scoring math. In VERDICT, our deterministic engine calculates the numbers, tests hard constraints, and models depreciation. The 120-Billion parameter cloud LLM only acts as an expert courtroom translator—explaining the verdict in plain language with quoted receipts."*

### SLIDE 5: Live Production Modules & Signature Features
* **Heading:** 04 / Core Features
* **Title:** Live Production Modules: Evidence Ledger, Reality Check & Market Intelligence
* **Key Modules:**
  * **Module A: Reality Check Guardrails:** Validates budget feasibility against live retail minimums before searching. Courteously rejects absurd requests (e.g. 'Car for ₹1' or '4K OLED for ₹5,000') and offers realistic used alternatives.
  * **Module B: Claims Cross-Examination Ledger:** Extracts seller claims and audits them against owner signals, classifying each as *Supported*, *Contradicted*, or *Unverifiable* with verbatim quotes.
  * **Module C: New vs Used vs Refurbished Matrix:** Compares brand new retail, certified pre-owned, and open-box items with depreciation curves, warranty delta, and lifespan scores.
  * **Module D: Price Radar & Sale Autopilot:** Audits MRP vs 90-day moving averages to catch fake discount hikes, tracks model refresh cycles, and sets automated calendar reminders.

> **Speaker Script:**
> *"Everything you see here is live and working. If someone asks for a car for ₹1, our Reality Check engine stops the search and explains realistic market minimums. Our Claims Ledger quotes real owners to challenge seller promises. And our New vs Used matrix reveals whether a refurbished model saves 40% with zero perceptible risk."*
